# Scopes

What changes when the same lesson set is applied to a different kind of spoken moment. Every
scope shares the read-aloud pass and the lessons; this file says what differs.

For each scope: who owns the WHAT (the content skill that runs first), the length band, the
register, where the belief turn sits, and what the listener must be able to do next.

| Scope | Content skill that runs first | Length | Register |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reel, Short, teleprompter read | `wdjm-script-writer` (belief, hook, proof, CTA) or `process-transcript` Step 3 (reels from a call). Their format and the 16 WDJM Script Writing Standards (Notion, Knowledge Resources hub) decide the shape. | 35 to 90 seconds, 120 to 250 words | Calm authority, one person talking to one person |
| Wedding-day MC and mic work | No content skill yet; Joe's own Wedding MC Mastery material is the voice source when it is distilled. Facts about the couple and the room come from Joe, never invented. | 5 to 40 seconds per announcement | Warm, in command, room-sized energy without shouting |
| Discovery-call and sales talk track | Joe's Sales Mastery knowledge in the Notion Knowledge Base (Source = Wedding Sales Mastery). The call is forty-five minutes; never call it short. | Openers 30 to 90 seconds, responses 10 to 30 seconds | Consultative, curious, unhurried |
| Talk, workshop segment, mentorship-call teaching, long-form narration | `produce-long-form` for To The Testimony; `process-playbook` lesson bodies for WDJM curriculum; otherwise the brief Joe gives | 90 seconds to 9 minutes | Teacher who has done the thing, principle first, story as proof |

## Reel, Short, teleprompter read

- The belief turn sits in the middle third. Hook earns the first five seconds, setup names the
  viewer's situation, the turn is one plain sentence, then the takeaway and the soft CTA.
- The listener must be able to repeat the belief back in one sentence and know the next step.
- Spoken text goes straight onto the teleprompter: no subheadings, no beat labels inside it.
  The WDJM teleprompter format in Notion is the authority.
- CTA is offer-state dependent. `process-transcript` carries the current rule (waitlist means
  point to the free community, no seat counts). Check it, do not default.

## Wedding-day MC and mic work

- The room is the audience, but the words are still addressed to one person at a time: the
  couple, then a guest, then the wedding party. "Everyone" is a last resort.
- Energy comes from pace, pause and certainty, not from volume or from adjectives.
- Names, titles and pronunciations come from Joe's planning sheet. A grand entrance script with
  a wrong name is worse than no script, so every name is a bracketed slot until Joe fills it.
- The belief turn is small and physical: what the room should do next (stand, cheer, look
  toward the doors, raise a glass). Say it once, plainly, in the last line.
- Never a joke at the couple's expense, never a line the couple has not heard before the day.

## Discovery-call and sales talk track

- Frame first, then curiosity. The opener sets what the next forty-five minutes are for and
  hands the talking to the couple. Joe listens more than he speaks.
- Reframes are questions the couple answers, not statements Joe wins. The reframe base template
  in `lessons.md` ends with a question for this reason.
- Objection responses acknowledge the real concern before anything else. A response that
  names a doubt the couple did not raise plants it (`joe-copy-standards`, rule 9).
- Nothing in a talk track promises an outcome, mentions scarcity, or calls the process a sale.
- Any story used as proof must be a real one from the verified registry or one Joe supplies.

## Talk, workshop, teaching segment, narration

- Principle first, then the story that proves it, then what the listener does with it. Joe's
  notes from Eli Wilde record four levels of teaching (tactics, strategies, principles,
  essence); a segment that stays at tactics is a tip, not a lesson.
- Every three to four minutes, a rehook or a participation prompt. A listener who has not been
  asked anything for five minutes is gone.
- Reveal levels apply to free content: the what, the why and the result are shared, the full
  how stays inside the paid room. `process-transcript` and `process-playbook` carry the rule.
- Narration for To The Testimony has its own laws in `produce-long-form` (length, contrast
  first, the fixed CTA). This skill adds the read-aloud pass, nothing else.
