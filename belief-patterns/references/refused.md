# Refused

Techniques from the sources that this skill will not apply, recorded so no future thread
re-adopts one by accident. Each collides with a Joe law (`joe-copy-standards`, the FTC rule,
the no-fake-scarcity rule, or rule 9 on planting doubt). The sources record where each came from.


- **Incentive for speed on the opportunity; "why now instead of waiting like unsuccessful
  people"; urgency trap questions; time bomb; law of contrast pricing ("$10k later, $2k now").**
  Manufactured urgency and fake scarcity. Source: EW-notes-2024-10-04 (Passive Vehicle
  Syntax), NLP-3.0-cards (cards 11, 15, 75, 100).
- **Creating doubt in the listener's beliefs on purpose ("how do you know your parents told
  you the truth"); fish out resistance and shoot it down; "get the prospect naked and needy";
  "break through their ego".** Manipulation, and the opposite of consultative. Source:
  NLP-3.0-cards (cards 46, 90, 147, 165).
- **Identity labelling to manufacture consistency ("you're the type of person who..." so they
  buy); consistency commitment boxes; embedded commands.** Pressure dressed as rapport.
  Source: NLP-3.0-cards (cards 29, 68, 77).
- **The "spanky" close, "I need to close you, this will change your life", "moral obligation to
  sell", "delusional belief everyone will buy".** Guru voice, and a promised outcome.
  Source: NLP-3.0-cards (cards 63, 78, 83, 84).
- **Vilifying the listener's current behaviour; making them feel something was missing "and
  it wasn't their fault" as a technique.** Rule 9: it plants the doubt to sell the cure.
  Source: NLP-3.0-cards (cards 95, 136).
- **Any line that promises a result, a number, or a timeline Joe cannot point to.** FTC rule
  and `joe-copy-standards` rule 6. Several cards carry these; none are used.
- **Authority tropes without a source.** At 17:50 in the drills video Eli reaches for a stat
  ("80% of tennis matches are spent not hitting a single ball", then "studies have shown,
  research has proven, the debate is over") and admits he cannot verify it. That moment is the
  fabrication ban in action. No "studies show" without a source Joe can name. Also "Now I just
  increased your dopamine" (18:30). Source: EW-drills-yt.
- **Consequence future pacing as pressure; "Do you really believe that, or are you just trying
  to be nice?"; labelling a room "victim story"; rewriting a listener's answer into "a good
  answer".** Pressure and verdicts. Source: EW-drills-yt (24:06, 32:14, 21:57, 29:48).
- **Eli's own credentials and prices.** "Nine figures", "100 million", course pricing. His
  facts, never in Joe's mouth. Source: EW-drills-yt.
