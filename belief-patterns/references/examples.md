# Examples

Worked examples per pattern in Joe's two worlds. Every line here is one of four things: Joe's
own words from his Sales Mastery or mentorship-call Knowledge Base entries (marked KB, with the
entry name), Joe's own notes from the Eli Wilde events, approved site copy, or a bracketed slot.
Nothing is invented. A line marked "shape" is a structure with the specifics left as slots.

## The reframe

**Joe's own, couple-facing (KB: Principle: Challenge "A Good Time" plus Reframe "Music" as
"Entertainment/Experience").** "Swap the word music for entertainment (or experience)
throughout the call. If you only say music, you're only putting value in the music." And the
good-to-great gap: "Are you just looking to have a good time, which honestly most decent service
will deliver, or are you hoping to hire someone who can make it a great time, an even better
experience for your guests?" That is "it's not this, it's that, and here's what it means" with
the confirming question built in.

**Joe's own, from his notes (2024-10-04), the "DJ" reframe.** Most couples think the DJ's job is
the music; what Joe does is run the whole experience, which means there is never a dead moment
for them or their guests.

**Joe's own, DJ-facing (KB: Objection: "You're out of our budget").** "All what she's doing is
giving you a fact. 'I'm outta the budget.' You as the salesperson have control of what that
means." The reframe of an objection into a statement of current understanding.

**Shape, DJ-facing (for a reel or a post):** "Most DJs think [the obvious thing] is what gets
them booked. What actually gets you booked is [the upstream thing], and here's what that means
for your next consult: [one concrete change]." Fill only from Joe's teaching, never from a
guess about what he teaches.

**The guard, in the source's words (EW-drills-yt 02:04):** the rep who "said money aside like
eight times ... she completely lost the frame." A reframe is connected to what the listener said
or it is nothing.

## Two sales, process before product

**Joe's own, couple-facing (KB: Framework: Get the Yes and Ask Why).** "Never reveal price until
they're sold on YOU regardless of price." Then: "Do you feel like I could be the right DJ for
you?" and, on the yes, "What makes you feel like I'm the right DJ?" Joe's note on why: "They are
revealing to themselves. You're not the one telling them that they need you. They're telling
themselves that they need you." The first sale is the couple's own belief about what the night
needs; the second is Joe; the price is logistics.

**Eli's own, for the shape (EW-drills-yt 04:56):** "I want the audience to believe that the life
they desire is on the other side of their communication skills." Then, and only then, the
program.

**DJ MOJOE site, the through line the first sale rests on (approved copy):** "A night your guests
keep talking about, and one you actually get to enjoy." Every section of the weddings page
argues that line before it argues for Joe.

**WDJM, shape:** the free community post installs a Core 6 belief ("the real problem isn't
gear"); the premium lesson teaches the process; the Accelerator is the product. Three layers,
never collapsed into one post.

## Question control and bridge questions

**Joe's own, couple-facing (KB: Framework: The Discovery Ladder).** "Fun means something
different to everybody. For you, what does fun look or feel like?" then "That's great you want
a packed dance floor, just curious, what's the main reason behind that?" then "How would that
make you feel?" Each question is the bridge to the next layer.

**Joe's own, the bridge on "we're talking to other DJs" (KB: Objection: "We're Talking to Other
DJs").** "How did those conversations go, did you like any of them?" then "So just for the fact
that we're speaking right now, is there something still missing that you're hoping to find?
What's kept you looking?" The bridge moves the focus from the other DJs to the gap.

**Eli's own, teaching (EW-drills-yt 10:49 to 11:14):** "So how do we actually do this? Well, if
you want to actually improve your communication, you need to first understand stagecraft. Now,
what do I mean by stagecraft? Stagecraft is how you capture and maintain attention."

**Written, shape (blog or sales page):** the H2 is the question the previous section left the
reader holding. "So what does the DJ actually control on the night?" is a bridge; "Our Services"
is a label.

## Tie-downs and wrapping the question

**Joe's own (KB: Framework: The Discovery Ladder):** "Comment before each next question ('Oh
100%, I'd do the same') so it feels like conversation, not interrogation." That is wrap the
question in Joe's words.

**Eli's own, tie-downs (EW-drills-yt):** "Does it make sense? Awesome." (04:51) "Are you with
me?" (10:49) "Cool?" (00:56). Spoken lanes only. On a page, none.

## Surface, source, self (three layers of pain)

**Joe's own (KB: Framework: The Discovery Ladder):** clarify the word ("fun"), find the why ("a
packed dance floor"), tie to emotion ("I'm an only child, this is my mom's moment too", quoted
in the entry as the kind of answer the why produces). That is surface, source, self, and Joe's
doctor analogy in the same entry is Eli's "describe their problem better than they can".

## Create the gap (describe their problem better, story arc)

**Joe's own (KB: Principle: Create the Gap):** "What we're doing here is creating a gap between
what they want and what they don't want. And then your solution fills that gap." Widen both
sides: the vision in detail, the fear in detail (train-wreck transitions, the cliché playlist,
the MC who goes rogue). The story arc's conflict and loss, in a consult.

## The close as a continuation, and honest urgency

**Joe's own (KB: Objection: "Let Me Think About It").** The golden bridge: "Definitely think
about it. Just before we hop off, so next time we speak I know to give you the right resources,
what would have to go through your mind, so I can share the right information to help you make
the best decision for your wedding entertainment?" Then the seven-day date hold, which is real
policy: "I hold the date for seven days, after that it's up for grabs. Would that work for you?"
Joe's own guard in the entry: "It only works if they're already sold on you. It's grace plus a
deadline, not pressure." That is the line between honest urgency and the refused kind.

## Trading, never discounting (guard on the budget objection)

**Joe's own (KB: Objection: "You're out of our budget").** Mirror ("Out of your budget?"),
isolate ("If you did have the budget, would we be working together?"), trade ("I'm happy to
honor a discount. In return, all I would ask is for a video testimonial after the wedding.").
The reframe is connected to their words, the urgency is absent, the exchange is real.
