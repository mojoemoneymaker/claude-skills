## Merge record: the Influence Architect prompt

Adopted as written or lightly reworded:
- The two layers, MINDSET and MOUTH, and the rule that every answer leaves Joe with words to say.
- The menu output: three intensities (soft, direct, full court), each with the line, the why, the delivery, the pushback line, then a risk note.
- The state protocol (purpose, self, person), the internal guardian posture, the one micro-adjustment after every interaction.
- Mode C, conflict and repair: de-escalate, own, reframe to us-against-the-pattern, propose, confirm.
- Practice mode: persona, resistance level, per-turn score with the pattern that fired versus the one that should have, stay in character until "debrief", debrief on the three deciding moments.
- Pre-frame (predict, validate, reframe), probability frame, as-if frame, why-not-lower, fear-versus-logistics, the "think about it" chunk-down, value isolation.
- Mode B rules: consequence and contrast aimed at the situation only, pacing before leading, the "win this moment or strengthen this relationship" check, the redirect when the real goal is control.
- Hard boundaries: hiding decisions from a partner, jealousy engineering, guilt traps, DARVO reversals, the connection-first rule.

Adapted:
- "Never buy their story about why they can't" became "their experience is real and you honour it; their conclusion is not final." The original reads as invalidation in Mode B.
- "Identity double commitment" (label plus consistent choice) is allowed only when the label names something they actually did. As a label to force consistency it is on Joe's refused list.
- "Always justify past failures, blame the old system" is allowed when true and never as a setup to sell the cure (Joe's rule 9 in `joe-copy-standards`).
- "Pressure is ethical if the offer serves them" became "real constraints and honest consequences, yes; pressure, no." Manufactured urgency is refused in every mode.
- The metaphor library: Eli's four plus the island kept as his; map, garden, fire alarm and two paddlers kept but labelled constructed, since they are not in any Eli source read here.
- "~80% of resistance lives in self-trust" restated as Eli's own line, "nearly 80% of every objection you get will be fear based."
- Emoji traffic lights replaced with words. Em dashes removed throughout (Joe's law).

Not carried:
- Nothing of substance was refused. The prompt's gaps were what this skill already had: the context intake, the per-domain allow and ban lists, what defeats each technique, confidence tags, the fabrication ban with bracketed slots, the isolation question and the objection protocol in Eli's words, types, and score calibration.
